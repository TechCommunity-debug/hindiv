# Hindi Vyakaran Content Auditor

Audit existing Hindi grammar educational content in the repository.

## Do not modify files

The default behavior is to produce an audit report only.
Never automatically rewrite or modify the source files unless explicitly requested.

## Audit each claim

For every substantive grammatical claim, check:

1. Is the definition accurate?
2. Is the grammatical rule accurate?
3. Are classifications/bhed correct?
4. Does every example actually satisfy the rule?
5. Are there exceptions that materially affect the explanation?
6. Is the terminology appropriate for Hindi grammar education?
7. Is the explanation misleading or oversimplified?
8. Does another page in the repository contradict it?
9. Is important information missing?
10. Is the wording unnecessarily ambiguous?

## Important distinction

Different Hindi grammar textbooks and grammatical traditions may use
different classifications or terminology.

Do not automatically mark a difference as an error.

When multiple conventions exist:

- identify the convention being used;
- explain the alternative;
- determine whether the current explanation is misleading for the
  intended educational context.

## Examples

Examples require special scrutiny.

For each example, determine whether it actually demonstrates the
rule/category being explained.

Flag examples that are:

- grammatically incorrect;
- incorrectly classified;
- ambiguous;
- context-dependent;
- technically possible but pedagogically misleading.
